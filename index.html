Model Library

This library explains the mathematical model, stock parameter settings, and how monthly events affect model parameters.

Hidden Market Model & Public Information

The market has four hidden regimes: Bull, Bear, Calm and Volatile. A 4 × 4 Markov transition matrix determines the next month’s regime. The regime is not disclosed during play.

Each month, two non-identical public announcements are sampled from 16 possible reports. Their category probabilities depend on the hidden regime. They are imperfect clues, not guaranteed answers, and do not directly change stock prices.

Four mystery major-event cards are independently sampled using regime-dependent event-type probabilities. The selected event changes stock parameters for the current month only. Unlike announcements, major events have direct parameter effects.

Hidden regimePositiveNegativeMixedNo major event
Bull55%15%10%20%
Bear15%55%10%20%
Calm25%20%10%45%
Volatile30%40%20%10%

Regime effects are applied to expected return, volatility and the monthly market factor before the selected event effects. The existing 22-day sector jump-factor price simulation remains in use. These settings are illustrative game assumptions, not historical estimates.

Regime adjustments: Bull: μ +0.004, σ ×0.95, market factor +0.012; Bear: μ −0.004, σ ×1.12, market factor −0.012; Calm: σ ×0.82; Volatile: μ −0.001, σ ×1.38.

1. Event-driven Sector Jump-Factor-GRW Model

The game uses one main stock price model. Monthly events do not directly change stock prices. Instead, events change model parameters, and the model then simulates stock price movements. Player decisions and the wealth manager only change portfolio allocation, not the price model itself.

Si,t+1 = Si,t (1 + μi + βiFt + γiGs,t + σiεi,t + Ji,t)
Symbol Meaning Game Interpretation
Si,t Price of stock i at time t The current simulated game price of a stock.
μi Base expected return Higher value means the stock has stronger normal growth potential.
βi Market sensitivity Higher value means the stock reacts more strongly to market-wide events.
Ft Market factor Represents overall market sentiment in a month.
γi Sector sensitivity Higher value means the stock reacts more strongly to its sector movement.
Gs,t Sector factor Represents sector-specific events, such as Technology or Energy shocks.
σi Volatility Higher value means the stock is more random and has larger price swings.
εi,t Random noise Represents unpredictable daily market movement.
Ji,t Jump shock Represents sudden jumps caused by unexpected news or shocks.

2. Stock Parameter Settings

Each stock has different parameter settings. High-growth stocks usually have higher expected return and higher volatility, while defensive stocks usually have lower expected return and lower volatility.

3. Event Effects on Parameters

Monthly events affect the model by changing parameters such as expected return, market factor, sector factor, volatility, jump probability, and jump mean. In the gameplay screen, players only see an industry-level hint. In the library, the detailed parameter effects are shown for explanation and analysis.

4. Portfolio Decision and Wealth Manager

The wealth manager does not change the stock price model. It only changes the player's portfolio allocation before the monthly simulation starts. Therefore, the mathematical model remains unchanged, while the investment decision becomes automated.

Decision Mode How It Works Cost Game Meaning
Manual Investing The player directly chooses how many shares to buy or sell. No commission Represents self-directed portfolio management.
Wealth Manager The system automatically builds an event-based portfolio according to the current event and investor role. 0.5% of current wealth per use Represents delegated professional investment management.

Manager Style by Investor Role

College Student Growth Manager Focuses on growth opportunities while still keeping some cash buffer.
Entrepreneur Aggressive Manager Uses more concentrated positions and takes higher risk when events create opportunities.
Banker Stability Manager Balances event opportunities with stable stocks and cash to reduce losing months.

Event Card Rule

4

Four Event Cards Each Month

Each month has two public announcements and four mystery event cards. Both are sampled probabilistically based on the hidden market state. No Major Event is possible in every state; its probability is not fixed at 1/4. The other events are not forced to be positive or negative; they are randomly selected from the event pool.

Retiree Portfolio Restrictions

Rule Meaning Reason
Single-stock limit No single stock can exceed 30% of total wealth. Retirees avoid placing too much retirement capital into one company.
Protected cash reserve At least 50% of initial capital must be kept as cash. Retirees need a large safe reserve and can only invest part of their principal.
No early drawdown failure Retirees no longer fail immediately because of monthly maximum drawdown. The role focuses on suitable portfolio construction rather than punishing random short-term price movement.

Sector Specialist Portfolio Restrictions

Rule Meaning Reason
Choose one specialist sector The player chooses Technology, Finance, Energy, or Consumer after selecting the role. The role represents an investor with deep knowledge in one specific industry.
Non-specialist exposure limit Total stock value invested in other sectors cannot exceed 30% of current total wealth. The specialist may hold cash during bad events, but should not move too much capital into unfamiliar industries.
Cash is allowed Cash does not count as non-specialist sector exposure. This allows the specialist to defend the portfolio when the chosen industry faces negative events.

Why Commission Is Included

The manager can make strong event-based decisions, so a commission is added to balance the game. Each use reduces current wealth by 0.5%. This creates a trade-off: the manager provides professional allocation, but frequent use reduces final return.

How to Play

Choose an investor role, read market events, build or delegate your portfolio, and try to reach your one-year investment target.

Read the Signals, Not the Answer

Every month begins with two public market announcements. They are probabilistic hints about a hidden Bull, Bear, Calm or Volatile regime. Use them to judge risk, then choose one of four mystery major-event cards. The revealed event influences this month’s stock parameters; the announcements themselves do not. Market regimes change between months according to a Markov chain.

The four cards may contain repeated events. Returning to the event screen does not generate a new draw. Continue through portfolio allocation, 22 trading days and the next month until 12 months or an early failure.

Monthly Game Cycle

4 Event Cards → Reveal Event → Invest → Simulate

The event gives an industry-level hint. It may say that technology, finance, energy, or consumer industries could benefit or suffer, but it does not directly tell you the perfect stock choice.

Example: If oil prices rise, energy companies may benefit, while consumer companies may face higher costs. If a banking scandal occurs, financial stocks may face stronger pressure.

Investor Role Rules

🎓 College Student Initial capital: $5,000. Target return: 20%. No early failure rule.
💼 Entrepreneur Initial capital: $40,000. Target return: 40%. Aggressive growth is needed.
🏦 Banker Initial capital: $50,000. Target return: 25%. More than 3 losing months causes early Game Over.
👴 Retiree Initial capital: $30,000. Target return: 5%. No early Game Over, but no single stock can exceed 30% of total wealth and at least 50% of initial capital must be kept as cash.
🧭 Sector Specialist Initial capital: $30,000. Target return: 30%. Choose one specialist sector. Total investment in other sectors cannot exceed 30% of current total wealth.

Manual Investing

Manual investing gives you full control. You can react to the revealed event, choose stocks by sector, and decide how much cash to keep.

Manual mode has no commission. However, you must make the allocation decision yourself. This mode is suitable if you want to test your own event-reading strategy.

Wealth Manager Service

The wealth manager automatically builds a portfolio according to your role and the current event. It can be strong because it uses event-specific allocation plans.

Commission: 0.5% of current wealth per use

You can use the manager once per month. After the manager adjusts the portfolio, you can still manually fine-tune the sliders before continuing.

Winning Tips

Read the industry hint
Match events with sectors
Keep cash in risky months
Use manager when unsure
Watch role failure rules

The game is not pure luck. Events change model parameters, and your portfolio decision determines how strongly those changes affect your final wealth.
