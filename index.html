Hidden Market Model & Public Information

The market has four hidden regimes: Bull, Bear, Calm and Volatile. A 4 × 4 Markov transition matrix determines the next month’s regime. The regime is not disclosed during play.

Each month, two non-identical public announcements are sampled from 16 possible reports. Their category probabilities depend on the hidden regime. They are imperfect clues, not guaranteed answers, and do not directly change stock prices.

Four mystery major-event cards are independently sampled using regime-dependent event-type probabilities. The selected event changes stock parameters for the current month only. Unlike announcements, major events have direct parameter effects.

Regime effects are applied to expected return, volatility and the monthly market factor before the selected event effects. The existing 22-day sector jump-factor price simulation remains in use. These settings are illustrative game assumptions, not historical estimates.

1. Event-driven Sector Jump-Factor-GRW Model

The game uses one main stock price model. Monthly events do not directly change stock prices. Instead, events change model parameters, and the model then simulates stock price movements. Player decisions and the wealth manager only change portfolio allocation, not the price model itself.

Si,t+1 = Si,t (1 + μi + βiFt + γiGs,t + σiεi,t + Ji,t)
Symbol Meaning Game Interpretation
Si,t Price of stock i at time t The current simulated game price of a stock.
μi Base expected return Higher value means the stock has stronger normal growth potential.
βi Market sensitivity Higher value means the stock reacts more strongly to market-wide events.
Ft Market factor Represents overall market sentiment in a month.
γi Sector sensitivity Higher value means the stock reacts more strongly to its sector movement.
Gs,t Sector factor Represents sector-specific events, such as Technology or Energy shocks.
σi Volatility Higher value means the stock is more random and has larger price swings.
εi,t Random noise Represents unpredictable daily market movement.
Ji,t Jump shock Represents sudden jumps caused by unexpected news or shocks.

2. Stock Parameter Settings

Each stock has different parameter settings. High-growth stocks usually have higher expected return and higher volatility, while defensive stocks usually have lower expected return and lower volatility.

3. Event Effects on Parameters

Monthly events affect the model by changing parameters such as expected return, market factor, sector factor, volatility, jump probability, and jump mean. In the gameplay screen, players only see an industry-level hint. In the library, the detailed parameter effects are shown for explanation and analysis.

4. Portfolio Decision and Wealth Manager

The wealth manager uses public announcements and Markov-chain probabilities to estimate the hidden regime, score stocks by expected return and risk, and allocate capital by investor style. It changes only portfolio allocation, never the underlying price model. The actual regime and future major event are not disclosed to the manager.

HMM Filtering & Risk-Adjusted Portfolio

1. Prediction: πt− = πt−1 T. 2. Exact news likelihood: Ls(a,b) = ws(a) × ws(b) / (1 − ws(a)) for two distinct ordered announcements. 3. Bayesian update: πt(s) ∝ πt−(s) Ls(a,b), normalised to sum to one.

4. Expected monthly direction: r̂i = Σs πt(s) [μi + Δμs + βi ΔMs]. 5. Risk proxy: σ̂i2 = Σs πt(s) [ (σi ks)2 + (ri,s − r̂i)2 ].

6. Stock score: Scorei = ar r̂i − av σ̂i + as Preferencei. 7. Cash target: clip(c0 + 0.20 pBear + 0.12 pVolatile − 0.08 pBull, cmin, cmax). 8. Stock weights: remaining stock budget is allocated using capped Softmax of the scores, with temperature τ.

Capital constraints are enforced: no leverage; Retiree keeps at least 50% of original capital as cash with a 30% single-stock cap; Sector Specialist keeps investment outside their specialist sector at or below 30% of wealth. Whole shares are purchased and remaining funds stay in cash. These are game assumptions, not statistically calibrated financial estimates. Event uncertainty is not explicitly priced into the manager's approximate score.

Four Event Cards Each Month

First, two public announcements are shown and the player invests. Only after the portfolio is confirmed and locked do four face-down cards appear. Their event types depend on the hidden regime. The player chooses one without seeing its content; there is no redraw or trade adjustment afterwards. No Major Event remains possible in every regime, and repeated cards are allowed.

Why Commission Is Included

The HMM-based manager can provide systematic probabilistic and risk-based recommendations, but it cannot predict the actual hidden regime or upcoming event with certainty. Each use costs 0.1% of current wealth (once per month); frequent use reduces final return.

How to Play

Choose an investor role, interpret market news, build and lock your portfolio, then draw an unknown event and evaluate your investment outcome.

Invest Before the Unknown Event

Every month begins with two public announcements generated under a hidden Bull, Bear, Calm, or Volatile regime. Use these imperfect clues to choose stocks or consult the HMM-based wealth manager. Only after you confirm and lock your portfolio do four face-down event cards appear.

Choose one unknown card to reveal the market event. It cannot be redrawn, and you cannot adjust your holdings until the following month. The chosen event changes stock simulation parameters, while announcements themselves do not. The 22-day market simulation then determines the outcome. Four event cards may contain duplicates.

The announcements provide imperfect signals, not a guaranteed forecast. You must commit to an investment before the major event is revealed, so risk management matters.

Example: If oil prices rise, energy companies may benefit, while consumer companies may face higher costs. If a banking scandal occurs, financial stocks may face stronger pressure.

Manual Investing

Manual investing gives you full control. Interpret public market news, choose stocks by sector, and decide how much cash to keep before the event is known.

Manual mode has no commission. However, you must make the allocation decision yourself. This mode is suitable for testing your own market-signal interpretation and risk strategy.

Wealth Manager Service

The wealth manager applies Hidden Markov Model (HMM) filtering to the same two public announcements you see, estimates the probabilities of four market regimes, then allocates capital based on expected returns, volatility, your role and cash protection rules. It does not know the true market state or which major event will be drawn.

Commission: 0.1% of current wealth per use

You can use the manager once per month. After the manager adjusts the portfolio, you can still manually fine-tune the sliders before continuing.

Winning Tips

Diversify before unknown events
